Safer places to borrow

  • Your bank or a cooperative bank.
    Small personal loans and overdrafts, with the cost written down before you sign.
  • Self-help groups (SHG).
    Group loans and bank linkage under the National Rural Livelihoods Mission.
  • PM SVANidhi.
    Working-capital loans for street vendors.
  • PM Mudra Yojana.
    Loans for small businesses and self-employed people, through banks and microfinance lenders.
  • Jan Samarth portal.
    One place to see which government credit schemes you may qualify for.
  • Your employer or family.
    A salary advance or a family loan can cost nothing.

Eligibility and terms change. Confirm with the bank or the official website.
